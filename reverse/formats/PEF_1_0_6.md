families not used by this particular executable.

## Main transition vector / TOC

The loader declares the main transition vector in section 1 at offset `0x2760`
(decimal 10,080).  After applying relocations against deterministic synthetic
section bases, it resolves to:

- code section offset: **`0x4D540`**;
- data/TOC section offset: **`0x8000`**.

Thus the original PowerPC entry routine begins at code offset `0x4D540` and
executes with **r2 = section-1 base + `0x8000`**.  This converts every normal
r2-relative TOC access into a concrete section-1 location and is the basis for
ongoing function/global/string correspondence work.

The entry routine at `0x4D540` immediately exposes conventional TOC-relative
loads and calls the earlier initialization path, independently corroborating the
transition-vector interpretation.

## Specification basis

The probe follows the packed-data and relocation definitions in *Inside
Macintosh: Mac OS Runtime Architectures*, Chapter 8, including the 7-bit packed
argument encoding, pattern-data opcodes, loader tables, and relocation
pseudo-machine.
